20-Year Hong Kong Local & Regional Expansion Timeline

2006
Central (中环) — Corporate Incorporation
TerraHash Energy Limited was incorporated at Two International Finance Centre (Two IFC) in Central, Hong Kong, establishing its global corporate headquarters and APAC power trade desk[cite: 1].
2007
Kowloon Bay (九龙湾) — Logistics & Procurement Center
Opened a specialized power grid procurement office in Kowloon Bay to handle cross-border transformer and high-voltage electrical equipment supply chains[cite: 1].
2008
Central (中环) — Institutional Project Debt Financing
Secured a US$120M credit facility structured through syndicated loans with major Hong Kong investment banks located in Central to fund Southeast Asian grid projects[cite: 1].
2009
Tsing Yi (青衣) — Energy Equipment Testing Facility
Leased industrial storage and testing facilities in Tsing Yi Port Zone to conduct quality control on high-capacity industrial circuit breakers before export.
2010
Admiralty (金钟) — Regulatory & Trade Advisory Hub
Expanded legal and regulatory compliance teams in Admiralty to manage cross-border energy tariffs and APAC clean energy trade frameworks[cite: 1].
2011
Wan Chai (湾仔) — Renewable Infrastructure Desk
Established a dedicated solar and wind farm asset management desk in Wan Chai, managing early-stage utility solar projects in Greater China[cite: 1].
2012

Reached 100MW in managed solar assets across Asia, with financial asset management operating directly out of the Two IFC headquarters[cite: 1].
2013
Shatin (沙田) — HKSTP Telemetry & Grid R&D Lab
