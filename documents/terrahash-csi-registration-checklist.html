CSI Registration & Prequalification Checklist

Internal development checklist for TerraHash Energy candidate projects being evaluated against New Jersey Competitive Solar Incentive program requirements.

01 · Project Classification

Confirm project type, capacity, site control and applicable CSI market segment.

02 · Site & Power

Document site characteristics, generation configuration, grid-supply or net-metered structure and interconnection pathway.

03 · Prequalification Package

Assemble required project information and supporting materials for prequalification review and bid preparation.

04 · Bid Readiness

Confirm commercial assumptions, schedule, technical configuration and internal approval before official submission.

TerraHash Energy Working Copy — Prepared with reference to the New Jersey CSI Program. Not an official NJCEP form. Official submissions must use the current forms and requirements issued by the program administrator.
