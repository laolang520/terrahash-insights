Competitive Solar Incentive program intelligence

A market structure that supports the TerraHash development model.

The New Jersey Competitive Solar Incentive program supports grid-supply solar, solar paired with storage and large non-residential net-metered projects. TerraHash Energy is using that framework as a working reference while advancing its own development review around generation, BESS, interconnection and flexible power demand. After this introduction, the program may be referred to as CSI.

Work underway

Development activity is being organized around project readiness.

Current work is centered on identifying candidate configurations, matching solar and storage architecture to local grid conditions, and evaluating which project pathways are suitable for deeper engineering and commercial review.

The objective is to move from market fit to site fit, then from site fit to development readiness.
