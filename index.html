CSI program intelligence

A market framework directly relevant to TerraHash Energy’s development model.

New Jersey’s Competitive Solar Incentive program is structured as an annual competitive procurement for qualifying grid-supply solar generation, grid-supply solar paired with energy storage, and large non-residential net-metered projects above 5 MW dc. The state program targets 300 MW of new solar generation annually and 160 MWh of storage paired with solar generation.

TerraHash development alignment

CSI creates a practical path for solar, storage and large-load infrastructure to be evaluated together.

For TerraHash Energy, the relevance is not limited to solar incentives. The CSI structure creates a clear development framework around project size, grid-supply participation, storage pairing and large non-residential load. That makes the program useful as a market reference for screening sites and infrastructure configurations that combine generation with controllable power demand.

The development question is no longer only whether a site can generate power. It is whether generation, storage, interconnection and flexible demand can be assembled into one bankable operating system.
