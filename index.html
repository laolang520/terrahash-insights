Current development activity

Grid capacity is driving site strategy

New Jersey is moving through a significant expansion of solar and energy storage while confronting hosting-capacity constraints on parts of its distribution system. TerraHash Energy’s current development work treats grid access as an early-stage site criterion rather than a downstream engineering issue.

The screening process considers the relationship between available power, potential generation, storage requirements and the operating profile of flexible digital infrastructure.

The development objective is to position flexible demand where power can be generated, stored and managed most effectively.

Storage is a core infrastructure layer

New Jersey’s storage market is moving into large-scale deployment. The state’s Garden State Energy Storage Program is designed around transmission-scale and distributed storage, reinforcing the role of BESS as a grid and development asset. TerraHash Energy is incorporating storage into its infrastructure model from the beginning rather than treating it as an add-on.
