2022 Strategic Overview: Financial Resilience & Enterprise Alliances

In 2022, amid market volatility, TerraHash Energy reinforced its position as a global leader through institutional capital reserves and corporate alliances. Headquartered in Hong Kong Central, TerraHash closed a $150 Million (HK$1.17 Billion) Series C funding round backed by conglomerates including New World Development (CVC) and BOCOM International. Concurrently, TerraHash partnered with energy giants NextEra Energy and AES Corp, headlined key global conferences in Miami and Austin, and expanded flare gas & geothermal sites in North Dakota and Iceland.

2022 新型能源扩展（油田伴气减碳 & 冰岛地热）

北达科他州威廉斯顿（Williston, ND）伴气利用：2022年中，TerraHash 在美国北达科他州巴肯（Bakken）油气区建设 40MW 伴气（Flare Gas）算力基地。将原本直接燃烧排放的废弃页岩天然气转化为电力，大幅降低甲烷与二氧化碳排放。


冰岛凯夫拉维克（Keflavik）地热能矿场：与冰岛地热发电厂合作，在凯夫拉维克扩建 30MW 100% 纯地热零碳数据中心，利用北极圈天然低温风冷技术，实现 PUE 低至 1.05 的极致散热效率。
